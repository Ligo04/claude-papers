Input Bodies

Each body is represented with 12 DOFs: a 3x3 affine matrix A (9 DOFs) plus a translation vector p (3 DOFs). Unlike rigid body dynamics with 6 DOFs and nonlinear rotation parameterizations, ABD uses a fully linear kinematic map: x = A * X + p, where X is the rest-space position. This linearity is the foundation that makes M-ABD efficient.
